FAQ

Questions? We have answers

What areas do you serve?

We provide residential cleaning in Calgary, with recurring and one-time services.

Do you offer recurring residential cleaning?

Yes. Choose weekly, biweekly, monthly or occasional cleaning.

Do you offer move-in and move-out cleaning in Calgary?

Yes, for homeowners, renters, landlords, realtors and property managers.

Do you clean short-term rentals or Airbnbs?

Yes. We help hosts get spaces ready between guests.

What is your cancellation policy?

Late cancellations incur a 50% fee. Maintenance cleans need 24 hours' notice, and move-in/move-out cleans need 3 business days' notice. Unsure? Email cleaningbyastrids@gmail.com.

Do you use environmentally friendly cleaning products?

Yes, where appropriate. We favour gentle, natural, eco-friendly products.

Are there things you do not clean?

Yes. We do not handle hazardous materials, biohazards, animal waste, pest infestations, unsafe areas or items needing specialized remediation.

Are you insured?

Yes.

How do I request a cleaning quote?

By phone, text, email or the contact form on this website. Estimates are free.
